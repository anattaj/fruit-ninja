# Fruit Ninja

Slice fruit out of the air with your finger. The page watches your webcam,
turns your index fingertip into a blade, and throws fruit and bombs at you.

Inspired by a reel from designedbyshreya.

Play it at https://anattaj.github.io/fruit-ninja/ - click "Play with webcam"
and allow the camera. Hand tracking runs in your browser; the camera feed is
never uploaded.

Read [STATUS.md](STATUS.md) for where it stands, and
[fruit_ninja.md](fruit_ninja.md) for how to run it, tuning, and known issues.
